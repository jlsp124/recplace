RECPLACE Professional Centre · Exterior design story

Professional presence comes from proportion, contrast, and a clearly marked arrival.

The exterior concept balances large glazed areas with charcoal and off-white planes. Orange accents mark key facade moments without overtaking the building’s black-framed composition.

Street-facing tenant zones sit within a consistent three-storey order, while the central glazed entrance gives the professional centre a single recognizable identity.
